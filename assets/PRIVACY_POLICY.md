# Privacy Policy for PulseRooms

**Last Updated**: October 31, 2025

## Introduction

PulseRooms ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
- **Account Data**: Email address, username, profile picture (via Clerk authentication)
- **User ID**: Unique identifier for your account

### 2. Mood & Emotional Data
- **Mood Pulses**: Your selected moods, intensity levels, timestamps
- **Mood History**: Historical record of your emotional patterns
- **Notes**: Optional text notes you add to pulses

### 3. Location Data
- **Approximate Location**: Your GPS coordinates when creating a pulse
- **City/Region**: Derived from GPS for territory features
- **How We Use It**: To show you nearby pulses and populate the global mood map
- **Anonymization**: Location data is rounded to the nearest 0.5 degrees (~50km) for privacy

### 4. Audio Data
- **Vibe Sounds**: 1-second audio recordings you optionally add to pulses
- **Storage**: Temporarily stored for 60 seconds, then automatically deleted

### 5. Social Data
- **Friends**: Users you connect with
- **Mood Circles**: Private groups you create or join
- **Sync Pulses**: Connection requests and acceptances

### 6. Usage Data
- **Analytics**: App interactions, feature usage, session duration
- **Device Info**: Device type, OS version, app version
- **Performance**: Crash reports, error logs

## How We Use Your Information

1. **Provide Core Features**
   - Display your pulses on the global map
   - Match you with PulseRooms
   - Connect you with friends via Sync Pulse
   - Track streaks and award badges

2. **Personalization**
   - AI mood analysis and insights
   - Mood forecasts for your area
   - Recommended friends with similar patterns

3. **Improvements**
   - Analyze app performance
   - Fix bugs and crashes
   - Develop new features

4. **Communications**
   - Push notifications for sync requests, streaks, challenges
   - Service updates and important announcements

## Data Sharing

### We DO NOT Sell Your Data

We never sell your personal or emotional data to third parties.

### We Share Data With:

1. **Service Providers**
   - **Convex**: Backend database (end-to-end encrypted)
   - **Clerk**: Authentication services
   - **Expo**: Push notifications
   - **PostHog**: Anonymous analytics (optional)

2. **Only When Required**
   - Legal obligations
   - Protect rights and safety
   - Business transfers (with notice)

### Public Data

The following is visible to other users:
- Your current pulse (for 60 seconds only)
- Username and profile picture
- Badges and achievements
- Activity in public PulseRooms

## Data Retention

- **Active Pulses**: Deleted after 60 seconds automatically
- **Mood History**: Stored while you have an account
- **Account Data**: Retained until you delete your account
- **Backups**: Kept for 30 days after deletion

## Your Rights

### You Can:
1. **Access**: View all your data via the app
2. **Export**: Download your mood history
3. **Delete**: Remove your account and all associated data
4. **Opt-Out**: Disable location services, analytics tracking
5. **Control**: Manage privacy settings for what's shared

### How to Exercise Rights:
- In-app: Settings → Privacy → Data Controls
- Email: privacy@pulserooms.app

## Location Privacy

- **Rounded Coordinates**: We round your location to ~50km radius
- **Optional**: Location is required for map features but you can use other features without it
- **Control**: Revoke location permissions anytime in device settings

## Children's Privacy
